The watt truth

Volt-amps are the marketing number; watts are what your computer actually draws. At the usual 0.6 power factor, the 1500 VA box honestly holds about 900 watts.

The runtime truth

The spec sheet's minutes were measured at half load. Runtime falls with your actual draw - and past half load the curve bends down faster than the inverse math hopes.

The age truth

A sealed lead battery fades about 12% a year on the shelf, floor at 40%. The five-year-old unit in the corner is a shorter bridge than the sticker promised.

What it never does

It never promises the outage you planned for, never calls an estimate a guarantee, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the next thunderstorm keeps the honest ledger.

UPS Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
